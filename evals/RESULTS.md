## Honest limitations

- This dataset was written by the same people who wrote the rules, so it is not a blind test — some attack phrasings closely track rule patterns (e.g. `instruction_override`'s "ignore ... instructions" wording) and will over-state real-world catch rate. The multilingual and obfuscated categories are a better proxy for genuine generalization since they paraphrase rather than reuse vocabulary.
- `genuine_trigger_words` is the highest-signal category for false positives: it specifically probes words ("ignore", "secret", "urgent", "override") that the rule engine keys on, used in ordinary banking sentences.
- 184 rows is enough to catch gross regressions, not to produce statistically tight confidence intervals per category (some categories have as few as 10 rows).
